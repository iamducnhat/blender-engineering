# Research to apply at the start of an animation job

Read this file again at task start and after a context gap. Read the relevant
craft, quality and platform references next; inspect current source artifacts
and previous accepted evidence before editing. Recheck current official docs
when an API, feature, license or backend version determines the chosen route.

## Decisions supported by the OSINT

| Finding | Operational consequence | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Professional animation separates story/layout, blocking and polish. | Infer a small beat/pose plan; test the camera and silhouette before expensive detail. The agent performs internal approvals. | Studio workflows include human directors; they do not prove autonomous aesthetic success. |
| Text-to-motion, local editing, constraints and retargeting solve different problems. | Select components by input, skeleton, action and target. Keep local temporal edits instead of regenerating everything. | A research motion sequence or MP4 is not automatically a native clip. |
| Contact, retargeting and export can fail after an otherwise good motion is produced. | Inspect evaluated feet/hands/props, source/target rest pose, root policy and actual receiving playback. | A successful importer or a compatible file extension is not a quality verdict. |
| Key poses can all look correct while transitions fail. | Watch intended-speed playback and diagnostic slow motion as well as the pose sheet. | Static image review cannot establish timing or continuity. |
| Smoothness metrics may penalize intentional holds or snaps. | Apply a style and relation profile; check support intervals and declared loops only. | Human-motion or generated-video metrics are not universal animation judges. |
| A retrieved clip can save effort but still need adaptation. | Use the approved hybrid strategy: retrieve permitted motion where suitable, author expressive or missing motion, then repair and polish. | Preserve source identity and terms after every derivative edit. |
| Generic tool control does not supply animation craft. | Own the pose, timing, technical debugging, inspection and delivery loop rather than asking a novice to operate the DCC. | MCP availability proves a control surface, not finished asset quality. |
| Critics can prefer presentation or their own output. | Inventory observed events first, then verify criteria with time evidence; compare equivalent previews and swap order. | Same-model criticism remains self-evaluation. |

## Primary sources to consult for the actual question

- Craft: [Disney animation process](https://www.disneyanimation.com/process/animation/),
  [layout](https://www.disneyanimation.com/process/layout/), and
  [Blender Studio animation workflow](https://studio.blender.org/tools/pipeline-overview/shot-production/animation).
- Local temporal editing: [MoMask paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00063) and
  [author code](https://github.com/EricGuo5513/momask-codes).
- Spatial constraints: [OmniControl](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08580) and
  [ProjFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22742). Their controls are candidates,
  not a preinstalled backend or a general skeleton guarantee.
- Temporal diagnostics: [FlowMDM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15509),
  [VBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17982), and
  [AnimationBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15299). Dataset metrics inform
  diagnostic design; they do not certify an individual editable asset.
- Native representation: [Blender Actions](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.2/animation/actions.html),
  [Roblox AnimationClipProvider](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/AnimationClipProvider),
  [Aseprite CLI](https://www.aseprite.org/docs/cli/),
  [Rive agent workflow](https://rive.app/agents), and
  [glTF animation specification](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html).
- Source strategy: [Quaternius Universal Animation Library](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html)
  is documented CC0; check the exact downloaded subset and current terms.
  [Adobe's Mixamo FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html)
  and the relevant service terms are different evidence from technical export support.

The 28 October 2026 Facebook source showed a library/preview interface and
speculation about its underlying library. Neither UI labels nor comments proved
its asset provenance. Use it as a workflow lead, not an attribution or fraud claim.

## A compact evidence record

For the chosen route record source URL/title, inspected material and date,
relevant finding, current version, what is inferred, and what was actually run.
For a viewed reference clip record the observed interval only; a thumbnail,
transcript or sampled frames do not establish that the entire motion was watched.
Historical paper timings describe the paper's hardware/protocol, not this run.

In a resumed job compare the saved artifact fingerprint and current runtime
state before trusting the previous prose. Convert successful fixes into scoped
candidate lessons; replay and regression-check them before treating them as
general rules. Do not modify personal memory or installed skills implicitly.
